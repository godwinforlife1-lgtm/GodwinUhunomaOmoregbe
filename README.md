## 🔬 My  Project

### Detection and Genotyping of High-Risk HPV DNA in Biobanked Cervical Samples
*M1 research project · Westend Multi-Specialist Hospital & Diagnostic Center, Warri, Nigeria*

I led a study investigating the detection and genotyping of high-risk Human Papillomavirus (HR-HPV) DNA in **150 biobanked cervical specimens** using the QuantGene 9600 real-time PCR platform.

- Overall HR-HPV prevalence of approximately **31%**
- **HPV-16** was the predominant genotype
- Highest positivity rate observed in the **21–25 age group**
